# Overview

Welcome to **Redshift**, a deep-space station management game. You command
**Gateway Station** on the edge of an asteroid field, and your goal is to grow it
from a bare command hub into a fully operational shipyard.

The game revolves around a simple loop:

1. **Mine** asteroids in the belt to gather raw materials.
2. **Store** those materials in your station's cargo hub.
3. **Research** blueprints in the Research Lab — new modules, ships, and upgrades.
4. **Build** the things you have researched, expanding the station and your fleet.

Everything you construct feeds back into the loop: more storage lets you stockpile
more materials, an Engineering Bay lets you manufacture ships, and a Power Core
keeps your high-draw modules online.

## The setting

You are stationed aboard *Gateway Station*, a Cygnia Corporation outpost. A single
support spacecraft — the **Tellus RX 5** — is docked and ready for deployment. The
asteroid belts around the station are rich in everything from common iron to exotic
antimatter, but nothing is refined until you go out and extract it.
