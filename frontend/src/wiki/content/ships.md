# Ships & Upgrades

Once the **Engineering Bay** is operational you can research and manufacture ships.
Each ship is its own blueprint branching off the Engineering Bay, and each ship has
a small set of **MK1 add-ons** that branch off it in the research tree.

## Ship stats

Every ship has five base stats:

- **Speed** — how fast it travels between the station and the belt.
- **Hull** — structural integrity (raw health).
- **Shield** — regenerating damage buffer.
- **Damage** — offensive output.
- **Manoeuvrability** — how tightly it turns and dodges.

Ships also track **condition**, **fuel** / **max fuel**, **fuel consumption**, and
**cargo capacity** for mining runs.

## The roster

### Tellus RX 5 — Support
A Cygnia Corporation support craft, and the ship you start with docked. Balanced and
dependable, with a roomy 150-unit cargo hold — your early mining workhorse.

- **Blueprint cost:** 50 Iron, 20 Titanium, 10 Silicates
- **Add-ons:** Engine, Stabilizer, Weapons

### Cygnus F-35 — Fighter
A high-precision strike fighter with balanced firepower, armor, and agility.

- **Blueprint cost:** 60 Iron, 25 Titanium, 12 Silicates
- **Add-ons:** Deflector, Stabilizer, Weapons

### Drax 22 — Interceptor
A light interceptor optimized for speed and quick engagements.

- **Blueprint cost:** 35 Iron, 10 Titanium, 8 Copper
- **Add-ons:** Engine, Stabilizer, Weapons

### Hammerhead 2 — Scout
A stealthy long-range reconnaissance scout — low signature, high sensor range.

- **Blueprint cost:** 30 Iron, 15 Silicates, 2 Gold
- **Add-ons:** Deflector, Stabilizer, Weapons

### Vanguard — Bomber
A heavy bomber: slow, but it carries devastating ordnance. The most expensive ship,
gated behind antimatter.

- **Blueprint cost:** 90 Iron, 40 Titanium, 3 Antimatter
- **Add-ons:** Engine, Plating, Stabilizer, Weapons

## Add-ons (MK1 upgrades)

Each add-on is researched separately, after its ship, and then attached. Not every
ship can take every upgrade — a ship only offers the add-ons that suit its role.

| Add-on | Effect | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine MK1 | Improves propulsion | 8 Copper, 4 Titanium |
| Deflector MK1 | Basic deflector array | 6 Titanium, 3 Silicates |
| Plating MK1 | Reinforced hull plating | 12 Iron, 6 Titanium |
| Stabilizer MK1 | Flight stabilizer system | 5 Copper, 2 Gold |
| Weapons MK1 | Basic weapons system | 10 Iron, 1 Antimatter |

Attached upgrades show up on the ship in the tactical view, so your fleet visibly
changes as you kit it out.
