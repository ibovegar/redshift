# Materials

Materials are the raw resources you mine from asteroids and spend on research and
construction. There are **ten** material types, ranging from common structural
metals to exotic exotics like antimatter.

## Material reference

| Material | Symbol | Storage cost / unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron | Fe | 2 |
| Carbon | C | 2 |
| Silicates | Si | 2 |
| Copper | Cu | 3 |
| Water Ice | H₂O | 3 |
| Titanium | Ti | 4 |
| Gold | Au | 5 |
| Uranium | U | 6 |
| Helium-3 | He³ | 6 |
| Antimatter | ā | 8 |

**Storage cost per unit** is how much of your station's storage capacity a single
unit of that material occupies. Heavier, rarer materials take up more room — a unit
of antimatter costs four times the space of a unit of iron — so capacity planning
matters once you start hauling exotics.

## Rarity tiers

Every deposit is graded by rarity. Rarer materials are harder to find in the belt
but are required for the most advanced blueprints.

| Tier | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Abundant structural materials (iron, carbon, silicates) |
| Uncommon | Useful metals (copper, water ice) |
| Rare | Higher-grade materials (titanium, gold) |
| Precious | Scarce, high-value (uranium, helium-3) |
| Exotic | Extremely rare (antimatter) — gated behind X-class asteroids |

## Where materials are used

- **Common metals** (iron, copper, carbon) build the early station modules.
- **Mid-tier metals** (titanium, silicates, gold) are needed for ships and add-ons.
- **Precious & exotic** materials (uranium, helium-3, antimatter) gate the Power
  Core and the heaviest warships like the Vanguard.

Keep a healthy buffer of common materials — you will spend them constantly — and
hoard exotics for the blueprints that specifically require them.
