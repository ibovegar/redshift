# Getting Started

When you first take command, Gateway Station already has a few systems online and a
stockpile of materials to bootstrap your progress.

## Your starting station

| System | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Command Module | Operational |
| Storage Hub | Operational |
| Research Lab | Available (ready to build) |
| Engineering Bay | Locked |
| Power Core | Locked |
| Storage Extension | Locked |

You also start with **1,000 units** of total storage capacity and a generous
opening stockpile of every material, so you can begin researching immediately.

## The core gameplay loop

1. **Open the Station Layout.** This is your build grid — every module the station
   can have is shown here, along with its current status.
2. **Build the Research Lab.** It is *available* from the start. Building it unlocks
   the rest of the research tree.
3. **Research a blueprint.** Pick a module, ship, or upgrade in the Research Tree.
   Researching costs materials and takes time.
4. **Build what you researched.** Once a module's blueprint is researched, its grid
   cell becomes *available* — spend the materials and wait for construction.
5. **Mine to refill.** Send your ship out to the asteroid belt to top up the
   materials you spent.

Repeat. Each new module opens up more of the tree and more of the station.

## A suggested first hour

- Build the **Research Lab**.
- Research and build the **Engineering Bay** — it gates the entire ship tree.
- Research the **Storage Hub** upgrades so you can stockpile more.
- Research your first **ship blueprint** and manufacture it in the Engineering Bay.

See **Station Modules** for what each section does and **Research** for how the
blueprint tree is structured.
