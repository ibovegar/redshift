# Interface & Controls

This page covers the main screens and how to get around them.

## Top navigation

The bar at the top of the screen has two buttons:

- **Home** — returns to the **Tactical** view, the live 3D scene of your station,
  ship, and the surrounding asteroid field.
- **Wiki** — opens this reference (where you are now).

## Tactical view

The Tactical view is the heart of the game — a real-time 3D scene rendering the
planet, sun, asteroid belts, your station, and your docked ship.

- **Pan** the camera to look around the scene.
- **Zoom** in and out to move between an overview of the field and a close-up of a
  single asteroid or your station.
- Asteroids you have scanned are marked so you can tell them apart from unsurveyed
  rocks at a glance.

## Ship status panel

The ship status readout shows your spacecraft's vitals at a glance using arc gauges
and bars:

- **Fuel** — remaining fuel against the tank's maximum.
- **Condition** — the ship's current structural condition.
- **Cargo** — how full the cargo hold is.

Keep an eye on fuel before sending a ship on a long extraction run, and on cargo so
you return to dock before the hold is full.

## Station Layout (build grid)

The Station Layout is your build interface. It shows every module the station can
have as a cell in a grid:

- **Operational** modules are listed in the always-visible module panel.
- **Available** modules show a **Build** button when you can afford them.
- **Locked** modules are dimmed until their prerequisites are met.
- The module currently under construction shows a live **progress bar** and a
  percentage readout; while a build runs, other Build buttons are suppressed.

Selecting a module opens its info panel with a description, cost, and — for modules
that produce something — an operational view of what it is doing.

## Research Tree

The Research Tree visualizes the blueprint dependency graph. Researched blueprints
are revealed progressively as you unlock their parents. Click a node to start
researching it (if you can afford it and its parent is done). The tree supports
**pan and zoom** so you can navigate the full graph.
